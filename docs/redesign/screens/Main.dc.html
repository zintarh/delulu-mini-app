Questions?

Do I need money to start?

No. Stake your word for free. Want to stake money? Verify once and claim free money every day from GoodDollar.

How are proofs checked?

AI checks your photo in seconds. You can add a friend as a witness if you like, but you don't have to.

What happens if I miss a day?

You pay up. Do the consequence you picked, or your money goes to the friend, charity or community pool you chose.

Why the face check?

It proves you're one real person, so rewards stay fair. It also unlocks free daily money, money stakes and 10× points.

Where does the free money come from?

GoodDollar gives verified people a small amount of free money (G$) every day. Keep it, or stake it on your habits.
